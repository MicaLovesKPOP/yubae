This is a good-faith, risk-managed publication decision, not a legal determination of fair use or a claim that silence constitutes consent. Functional keyboard metadata is also MIT-compatible. Earlier MIT grants remain valid. None of these grants purport to license the underlying Exile Zone language, reconstructed glyph artwork, font outlines, third-party material, or the show and its trademarks. In particular the reconstructed artwork in source/glyphs.py, chart-derived third-party expressions in data/ and bundled glyph font are not granted MIT, GPL, CC BY-SA or OFL rights by these notices. Generated packages combine separately scoped materials. For official catalogue distribution, confirm Keyman's licensing requirements and resolve the relevant asset rights; do not represent the font as OFL without a valid basis.

## Corrections and rights concerns
Rights holders and contributors can contact the maintainers through the repository's Issues page, or GitHub's copyright reporting process for sensitive matters. Please identify the affected files or release and the requested correction or removal; do not post confidential personal information. We will promptly review credible claims, suspend or replace affected downloads where appropriate, correct attribution and remove or revise disputed material. Maintain a compatible migration path where possible. Do not argue that silence or prior public availability proves permission. External outreach and new paid services still require owner approval.
